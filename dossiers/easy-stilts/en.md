# Stilts workshops — for children, young people and adults

**AGES 8+ · 2 HOURS · UP TO 20 PEOPLE**

Stilts are long poles that you tie to your legs.
On stilts, a person is very tall.
In this workshop we learn to walk on stilts.

## Workshop for children

- Ages 8 and up.
- Up to 10 children.
- It lasts 2 hours.
- We learn to put on the stilts.
- We learn to walk and dance to music.
- At the end we dress up and do a parade. A parade is a walk with music.

## Workshop for young people and adults

- Ages 8 and up.
- Up to 20 people.
- It lasts 2 hours.
- We learn to walk, to fall and to get up again.
- We dance and play games.
- We create a character.
- At the end we do a short show for the audience.

## What we need

- A space of 10 by 10 metres.
- Flat, hard ground. No gravel.
- Indoors, the ceiling must be 4 metres high or more.
- Shade and drinking water.
- Comfortable clothes and flat, closed shoes.
- We bring the stilts and the costumes.

## For everyone

We have experience with groups of disabled people.
We adapt the workshop to the group.

## With a show

We give the workshops on the same day as one of our shows.
We come from Barcelona.

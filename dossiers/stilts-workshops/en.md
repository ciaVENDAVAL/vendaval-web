# Stilts workshops

**AGES 8+ · 2 HOURS · UP TO 20 PARTICIPANTS · NO POWER NEEDED**

The circus artists of cia. VENDAVAL share their passion for stilts with young people and adults. We bring stilts workshops to festivals, schools, civic centres and events.

Stilts are individual stages that lift each person up. Together, the participants become a moving set of performers who can wander to any corner of the venue. Almost anything you can do on the ground, you can do on stilts: dance, sing, play an instrument, play the fool, even easy acrobatics.

Up on stilts, a street or square becomes a meeting place: people greet each other, play and dance together, and the venue fills with colour.

*With the support of Tub d'assaig*

## Kids' stilts workshop

### Synopsis

Climb up and see the world from above! In this workshop, children learn to walk on stilts and dance to live accordion music. To finish, we put on costumes and parade through the venue together.

### What will we learn?

- Putting on stilts safely, finding our balance and taking our first steps
- Walking, turning and dancing to music
- Moving together as a group
- Dressing up and parading through the venue

### Details

- **Ages**: 8 and up
- **Participants**: up to 10
- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Instructors**: tyle fernández, with Vic Heredero or Albert Pérez
- **Parents**: outdoors, if there is nothing to hold on to (trees, a fence, a wall), a parent or guardian helps each child for the first 20 minutes
- **Final parade**: route agreed with the organiser beforehand

## Stilts workshop for youth and adults

### Synopsis

Learn to walk and dance with longer legs and free hands! First we become independent on stilts. Then we explore group movement, tricks and characters, and create a short presentation for the public.

All of this happens in a relaxed, trusting atmosphere where safety and mutual support come first. We help each other so that everyone learns something new and leaves behind the fear of heights, of falling or of performing in public.

### What will we learn?

- Putting on stilts safely, balance, falling and standing up again
- Walking, turning, dancing and moving to live music
- Group movement and choreography
- Simple tricks and games
- Building a character
- Creating and performing a short presentation for the public

### Details

- **Ages**: 8 and up, mixed groups of youth and adults welcome
- **Participants**: up to 20; 10 on stilts at a time, while the others assist and spot, then swap
- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Instructors**: tyle fernández, with Vic Heredero or Albert Pérez

### Longer formats

- Intensive weekend course
- Weekly course over several months

Ask us for details.

## Technical sheet (both workshops)

- **Space**: about 10 × 10 m.
- **Ground**: flat and firm: street, square, park, sports hall. Dirt is fine; gravel is not.
- **Indoors**: minimum height 4 m, overhead lighting.
- **Sound**: our own Bluetooth speaker and live acoustic instruments (accordion). No power needed.
- **For participants**: shade and drinking water nearby.
- **Clothing**: comfortable clothes and flat, closed shoes.
- **Materials**: stilts, costumes and everything else are provided by the company.
- **Instructors**: the number depends on the number of participants.
- **Rain and wind**: in rain, the workshop is postponed or moved indoors. Wet ground is fine only with no standing water. Mild wind is fine.

## For all bodies and minds

VENDAVAL wants to reach everyone, including differently abled people. Our instructors have experience with mixed-ability groups. Tell us about your group and we will adapt the workshop.

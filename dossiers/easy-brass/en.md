# VENDAVAL Brass — music and circus in the street

**8 ARTISTS · 30 MINUTES · STREET · ALL AGES**

VENDAVAL Brass is a band that walks through the street.
There are circus artists with the band.

## Who we are

- 6 musicians: 3 saxophones, 1 trumpet, 1 sousaphone and 1 drummer.
- 2 circus artists: 1 on stilts and 1 on a unicycle.

Stilts are long poles on the legs. On stilts, a person is very tall.

A unicycle is a bike with 1 wheel.

## What happens in the show

- The band plays music for dancing.
- The circus artists juggle.
- The audience plays with nets and life rings.
- The band freezes like a statue. The audience wakes the musicians up.
- The audience learns hand signs. With the signs, the audience leads the band.

## When and where

- In a street or a square.
- By day or at night.
- Each show lasts 30 minutes.
- We rest for 30 minutes between shows.
- No technicians needed.
- We cannot play in the rain. We can play later or on another day.

## Concert in the audience

We also play a concert with microphones.
The concert is on a square.
The band moves among the audience.

## For everyone

- Before the show, blind people can touch the costumes and the instruments.
- If the organisers have the equipment, we can also offer audio description, sign language and vibrating backpacks.

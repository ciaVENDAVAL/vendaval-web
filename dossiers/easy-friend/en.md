# Imaginary Friend — a giant soft toy

**1–2 ARTISTS · 30 MINUTES · STREET OR INDOORS · ALL AGES**

Imaginary Friend is a giant soft toy.
The soft toy walks through the street.
It looks for friends to play with.

## What it looks like

The soft toy is very tall.
It walks on 4 legs.
Inside there is a person on stilts.
Stilts are long poles on the legs.

## 3 ways to do the show

1. **With acrobatics**: an acrobat wants to sleep. First they stretch, jump and dance with the audience. At the end they sleep on top of the soft toy.
2. **With music**: a musician plays a wind instrument.
3. **The soft toy alone**: the soft toy plays with the audience.

## What we need

- A street, a square or a park.
- Indoors, the ceiling must be 4 metres high or more.
- A place to change, with water.
- No technicians needed.
- If it rains, we do the show later or indoors.
